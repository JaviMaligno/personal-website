# Holidays that come back every year

Most holidays fall on the same date every year, and listing them again for each year
is tedious. An entry in a schedule's `holidays` list may now also be a month and a
day, `MM-DD` (for example `12-25`), meaning that day in every year.

Full `YYYY-MM-DD` entries keep meaning that one day only, and both kinds can be mixed
in the same list. Everything else about holidays stays as it is.
