# Business Landing - Market Validation Strategy

## Purpose

This landing page (`/en/business`, `/es/business`) tests the viability of offering automation services to non-technical SMEs (small and medium enterprises), as a potential expansion from the current technical audience (CTOs, engineering teams).

## Hypothesis

- The SME market is larger and has more repetitive problems that are easier to productize
- Non-technical business owners will pay for automation if positioned as time/money savings rather than technical features
- This market could provide a path to scalable products (templates, SaaS) rather than custom consulting

## Target Audience

**Primary**: SME owners and business managers who:
- Spend significant time on repetitive administrative tasks
- Experience errors from manual processes
- Don't have technical background but recognize inefficiency
- Are open to external help for automation

**Verticals to test** (via segmented ads):
- Healthcare clinics (appointment scheduling, patient follow-up)
- Real estate agencies (lead tracking, property research)
- Marketing agencies (reporting, data compilation)
- Professional services (document generation, client onboarding)

## Messaging Strategy

### Core Message
"Automate the tasks that steal your time"

### Key Value Propositions
1. **Time savings**: "Recover 10+ hours weekly"
2. **Decision fatigue**: "Every decision counts" - automate the trivial to focus on what matters
3. **Error reduction**: Eliminate manual mistakes

### Differentiation from Main Site
| Main Site | Business Landing |
|-----------|------------------|
| Technical language (MCP, agents, pipelines) | Benefits language (save time, reduce errors) |
| Architecture diagrams | Simple 3-step process |
| Developer-focused projects | Reframed use cases (research, documents, analysis) |
| Multiple CTAs | Single CTA to Calendly |

## Reframed Projects

Technical projects repositioned for business audience:

| Original | Business Version | Use Case |
|----------|------------------|----------|
| Data Source Automator | Automatic Research | Compile info from multiple sources |
| Industry Classifier | Client Evaluation | Classify and assess clients/suppliers |
| Job Hunting Pipeline | Smart Search | Find and filter relevant information |
| Book Corrector | Document Review | Automatic document correction |
| LTV Calculator (Insurance) | Customer Analysis | Analyze customer data for decisions |

## Validation Plan

### Phase 1: Traffic Test
- **Budget**: ~500€ in ads
- **Duration**: 2-3 months
- **Channels**: Google Ads, LinkedIn (test both)
- **Approach**: Segmented ads by vertical, all pointing to same landing

### Metrics to Track
1. **Cost per lead** (Calendly booking)
2. **Lead quality** (discovery call show rate)
3. **Conversion rate** (call → proposal)
4. **Average ticket size**
5. **Which verticals perform best**

### Success Criteria
- Cost per qualified lead < €50
- Discovery call show rate > 70%
- At least 1 closed deal in test period
- Clear signal on which vertical to double down

### Phase 2: If Validated
1. Create vertical-specific landing pages
2. Develop productized offerings (fixed-price packages)
3. Build repeatable solutions for common problems
4. Consider migration to subdomain (`business.javieraguilar.ai`)

## Technical Notes

### No Navigation Link
The business landing is intentionally NOT linked from the main site navigation. It's designed for paid traffic only, to:
- Keep audiences separate
- Measure ad effectiveness accurately
- Avoid confusing technical visitors

### Future Migration to Subdomain
If validated, the landing can be moved to `business.javieraguilar.ai` with:
- Same codebase (Astro handles both)
- Vercel subdomain configuration
- Potentially separate header/footer for stronger brand separation

## Pricing Strategy (for discovery calls)

No prices shown on landing - discussed in calls to:
1. Learn what the market will pay
2. Adapt pricing to each case
3. Gather data on budget expectations

Initial guidance for calls:
- Small automation (single process): €500-1,500
- Medium project (multiple processes): €1,500-4,000
- Ongoing retainer: €500-1,500/month

## Long-term Vision

If SME market validates:
1. **Short term**: Custom consulting with repeatable solutions
2. **Medium term**: Productized packages by vertical
3. **Long term**: SaaS product for common automation needs

This path offers better scaling potential than purely custom technical consulting.
