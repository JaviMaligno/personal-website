Do the AI models that win benchmarks do better work? I tested it three times.

Exam questions with a twin, same method and new numbers: no model stumbled. Seven models running their own agents: six got everything right. Then nineteen things I had really asked an agent to do, with no answer key: six models needed about three of my corrections per task, DeepSeek four, and none got it right on its own.

What mattered far more was the task, and how I asked for it. When I phrased a request as a question, the models answered it and stopped: 40 times out of 49.
