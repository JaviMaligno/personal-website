I erased the ball from the pitch and kept only the players. Can you tell where it is?

My four-part Where's the Ball? series, in 50 seconds.

On the hard cases, frontier vision-language models did no better than a coin flip. A 60 kB network trained on my laptop beat that bias 82% of the time, and its trick is almost embarrassingly simple: look where the running players are heading.

Which is also why the hardest ball to find isn't the one in flight. It's the one standing still. No motion, no signal.

The data, the caveats and the parts where I almost concluded the wrong thing are in the articles.
