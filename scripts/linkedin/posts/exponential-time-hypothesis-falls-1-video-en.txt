Is there one guest list that meets every friend's conditions?

That dinner party is 3-SAT, the reference hard problem of computer science. Checking a list is easy. Finding one by trying them all doubles in cost with every friend you add. In 1999 that became a bet, the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and more than 800 papers rest on it.

Last week two AI labs proved it false, each with a proof checked in Lean. This is part 1 of 2: the problem. Part 2, how they got through, comes on Saturday.
