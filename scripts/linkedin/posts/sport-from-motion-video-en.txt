Ten dots. No pitch, no ball, no kits. Which sport is it?

I took real tracking data from four sports and kept only how the players move. A small classifier, trained in minutes on my laptop, gets the sport right 83% of the time. Five frontier models, on the same clips, land between chance and 47%, and most fall back on a favourite sport when unsure.

Then I shuffled the snapshots. If you read motion, order matters. Only Claude Opus 5.5 lost clearly, 10 points. For the other four there's no evidence they use the order, which isn't proof they don't.

The dots at the start of the video? Basketball. Three models said American football.
