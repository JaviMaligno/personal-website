Can AI invent a new idea by picking an empty spot on the map of everything that exists?

A tweet proposed exactly that: map every game on Steam, pick points far from all of them, and turn each one back into a game. A friend said image models already do it: ask for an astronaut riding a horse and you get one. But they get there by mixing things they know. Far from everything, there may be nothing to mix.

So I tested it on games, papers and patents. Show a model the nearest games and it just blends them: a black cat, some hamsters and a furry stepmom became a kitten pushing blocks. Search with no neighbours at all and the best coherent text I found sits at 0.14 from a point at 1. Texts live on a narrow cap of the sphere, and far points sit outside it.

What did work was asking with no map at all, as long as the model could see the ideas it had already proposed.
