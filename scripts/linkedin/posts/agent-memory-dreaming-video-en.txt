Your AI can remember the wrong thing.

Anthropic illustrated this with Claude playing Pokémon: a mistaken note kept sending it the wrong way. Reviewing the original session record corrected the note and changed the next decision.

This explainer follows that idea through what deserves saving, linked notes, and checking a world that has changed.

The game scenes are illustrations. Sleep is a metaphor for offline memory review.
