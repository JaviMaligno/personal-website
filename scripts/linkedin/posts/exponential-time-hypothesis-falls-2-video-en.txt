Two AI labs refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis. Here is how.

The wall: hard formulas can't be split without cutting too many links. Both labs go through it the same way. They count the solutions instead of deciding whether one exists, so terms can be negative and cancel.

Claude's proof uses an identity from 1818, from the octonions. OpenAI's deforms the problem, solves the easy cases and interpolates back. No faster solvers: it only wins past 10^10,000 variables. Both proofs are checked in Lean; the human review is under way.
