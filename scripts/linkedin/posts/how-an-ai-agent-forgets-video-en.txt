Your AI said "got it". An hour later, it booked the old plan.

It didn't ignore you. It forgot. An AI has a few places to remember things: the chat it can see, a summary, a searchable archive, a few named boxes. Each one forgets in its own way, and in my tests with AI agents, the one that sounds safest did the worst: a keyword search almost never looks for a correction it doesn't know about.

When something matters, ask it to save it to memory.
