A newer model was faster and looked just as good. I didn't switch.

Picking a model isn't about finding the best one. It's about the frontier: the options nobody beats on cost, speed and quality at once, measured per task rather than per token. Then a quality floor, and a price on your seconds.

In my case: ten times the price per token, 23% more per task, 27 seconds faster. That's about $3 per hour of waiting. Here, it wasn't worth it.
